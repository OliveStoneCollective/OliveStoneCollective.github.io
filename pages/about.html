About Our Company

MAKE YOUR KITCHEN A CREATIVE SPACE

Who we are:Founded in Colorado, our company was created from a love for cooking and the joy of making the kitchen feel like a fun, creative space. We know that everyday cooking can sometimes feel repetitive, overwhelming, or uninspired, especially when you don’t have the right flavors or kitchen essentials on hand.

What started as a passion for experimenting with flavors, ingredients, and kitchen essentials has grown into a brand focused on bringing inspiration, style, and simplicity into everyday cooking. With thoughtfully selected seasonings and kitchen essentials, Olive & Stone makes it easier to turn ordinary meals into something you can look forward to creating.
